ADD BACKGROUND MUSIC HERE (optional)
====================================

1. Copy one audio file into this folder (public/music/) and name it:

       track.mp3

   (Or use any name/format and update the path in  src/data/site.js
    under features.music.src — e.g. "/music/my-song.mp3".)

2. That's it. A small floating music button appears at the bottom-left.
   Music NEVER autoplays — the visitor must press play.

To turn music OFF completely:
   Open  src/data/site.js  and set  features.music.enabled = false
